[b][size=150]QUARANTINE ZONE — ESSEKER[/size][/b]

A survival sandbox for Ravage on the Esseker map. Dynamic missions, ambient
events, and a hostile Renegade faction that builds, patrols, and reacts.
Play solo or up to 8 players co-op.

[b][size=125]FEATURES[/size][/b]

[list]
[\*][b]3 mission types[/b] — Rescue, Investigate, and Cache. They spawn in
batches of 3. Complete all three, a new batch generates automatically.
[\*][b]3 ambient events[/b] — Helicopter patrol, supply drop, and hostile
roadblock. Random scheduler, no repeats in a row.
[\*][b]Persistent 3-man AI squad[/b] — Each player gets a personal fireteam
that survives respawns. Same names, faces, and classes every time.
[\*][b]Dynamic Renegade base[/b] — A hostile camp spawns every ~25 minutes
near a random town. 20 bandits, patrols, watchtowers, and a loot crate.
[\*][b]Reactive reinforcements[/b] — Attack the base and a second squad
flanks from a random direction. They don't count toward base clear.
[\*][b]Clearable and lootable[/b] — Wipe the garrison and the base
disappears. A supply crate stays behind as your reward.
[\*][b]Roaming squads[/b] — Every 20-30 minutes a 5-man bandit patrol
leaves the base and heads toward an active mission.
[\*][b]ACE Arsenal storage[/b] — Four categorized crates at base HQ.
Weapons, gear, ammo, and attachments.
[\*][b]5 loadout kits[/b] — Automatic rifleman, light AT, medic, rifleman,
and a bandit kit for the AI.
[/list]

[b][size=125]HOW IT PLAYS[/size][/b]

You start at HQ with a 3-man squad and standard kit. Missions appear
in your task list with markers on the map. The Renegade base does
not — it's announced by name only. You'll have to find it.

The world is hostile but not overwhelming. Events fire on their own
schedule. Something is always happening, but you rarely get swarmed.
The pacing is meant to feel like a quiet survival scenario with
moments of threat, not a constant firefight.

[b][size=125]NOTES[/size][/b]

[list]
[*]Recommended for 1-4 players. Tested up to 8.
[*]Not a hardcore survival scenario — loot is reasonably generous so
you can focus on the missions.
[*]The Renegade base is a mid-to-late objective. Early on, avoid it.
[*]Zombies (Ravage infected) and Renegades are separate threats.
Renegades are East-side, so they'll shoot infected as well as you.
[*]Two terrain locations are excluded from mission and event spawns:
Gromada and Lower Esseker.
[/list]

[b][size=125]KNOWN LIMITATIONS[/size][/b]

[list]
[*]The Renegade base is not marked on your map. This is intentional —
you're meant to scout for it. Listen for the chat message about its
location.
[*]AI pathfinding on Esseker's urban areas can occasionally cause
defenders to stall inside buildings. Rare, but possible.
[*]The supply drop cargo net is a physical object. Don't stand under it.
[/list]
